# 禾多人生遊戲入口

## Platform

web

## Audience and purpose

Players open standalone browser games from the 禾多人生 game lobby. Each game can take an episode or a recognizable character into its own genre.

## Product commitments

《資料科學大戰》 follows the original Friday data science meeting. The player joins by accident. The opening draws three items and the player carries one. Sam starts at zero anger, asks why the player entered, then asks three randomly sampled questions from an eleven-question bank before Joe challenges him. Ethan sings throughout, Jack hides a laugh until the first danger signal, and Wine automatically gives cola and chips once at the first anger threshold. Solving issues while retaining trust and keeping the meeting calm leads to the best ending. A confirmed duel resolves the meeting. Traditional Chinese dialogue uses fictionalized writing and drawn characters. Island battles and this game's local save stay independent of career progress.
