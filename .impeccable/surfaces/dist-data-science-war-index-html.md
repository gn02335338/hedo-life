# 資料科學大戰

Mode: Experience. A browser game for the 禾多人生 lobby. The Friday story leads and contemporary debates interrupt it.

## Direction contract

**THESIS** A meeting projector becomes a visual novel stage. Players must connect a spoken claim with a piece of evidence before challenging it.

**OWN-WORLD** After-hours office cel art, aubergine shadows, hot coral interruption and manila evidence slips. The scene is dark and the paper is bright, like a single light spilling from a projector.

**STORY** An accidental guest becomes the meeting’s moderator, challenger or quiet witness. The others bring their own positions, with Jack mediating. The player first takes one of three dealt items and keeps trying to advance the meeting. A confirmed Island duel ends the meeting.

**FIRST VIEWPORT** A narrow dark lobby bar above a wide illustrated room. A door-side table shows three item choices after the cover. The first debate scene then shows a speaker and transcript on the left and selectable paper evidence on the right. The light becomes more coral as the temper rises.

**FORM** Graphic office anime, pinned by the brief’s visual-novel reference, executed in code with original art. seed:f3f7971fb786fb75.
